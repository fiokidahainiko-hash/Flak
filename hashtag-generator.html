Hashtag Strategy Tips

Use these proven tricks to make your hashtags actually work.

🎯 Mix sizes

Use a mix of huge, medium and small hashtags. Small ones (under 100k posts) are easier to rank in.

⚡ 3 to 5 is plenty

You don't need 30 hashtags. Three to five well-chosen ones often outperform a wall of hashtags.

🎧 Use the trending sound's tags

Tag the trending sound's niche along with your content tags to ride the algorithm wave.

🔄 Rotate weekly

Change your hashtags regularly so TikTok sees your content in different search pools.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags should I use on TikTok?

Most top creators use between 3 and 5 hashtags, though 10 to 15 can work for discovery-heavy niches.

Do hashtags actually increase views?

Yes, but content quality matters most. Hashtags help TikTok understand your topic and show it to the right audience.

Can I reuse the same hashtags?

You can, but rotating them keeps your content fresh in search and discovery feeds.

Are niche hashtags better than big ones?

Usually yes. Niche hashtags have less competition, so your video can rank higher and get more views per post.
