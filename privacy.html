Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Flakcreatorstudio ("we", "our", "us") operates the website fiokflow.online and the Flakcreatorstudio creator toolkit. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who We Are

Flakcreatorstudio is operated by Fiokidaha Usen Iniko, a software developer based in Nigeria. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at Fiokidaha@fiokflow.online.

2. Information We Collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. The website processes:

  • TikTok URLs you paste — used only to fetch the video/audio you request and immediately discarded. We do not store your links or downloaded files.
  • Automatically collected data — standard web analytics such as pages visited, device type, browser, approximate location and time of visit, collected by Google Analytics.
  • Cookies — used by Google Analytics and Google AdSense to deliver and measure ads and to personalise advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate our downloader and creator tools.
  • To measure site performance and improve our content (Google Analytics).
  • To display relevant advertising (Google AdSense) which helps keep our tools free.
  • To respond to your enquiries when you contact us.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services that operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — usage analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense — advertising, including personalised ads and cookies. See Google's Advertising Policy.

As a Google AdSense publisher, we use cookies to serve ads, including personalised ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to understand how visitors use the site and to serve ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect how some features work.

6. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We only share data with third-party processors listed above as necessary to operate the site. We apply reasonable technical measures to protect any information handled through the services.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, including under the Nigerian Data Protection Act (NDPA), you may have the right to access, correct or request deletion of personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, email Fiokidaha@fiokflow.online.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at Fiokidaha@fiokflow.online.
