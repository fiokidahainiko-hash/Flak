How To Convert A TikTok Video To MP3

Turn any of your TikTok videos into an MP3 in three easy steps.

  1. Open TikTok, go to the video whose audio you want, tap Share and Copy Link.
  2. Paste the link into the converter box above.
  3. Tap Convert to MP3, then Download MP3 to save the audio file.
