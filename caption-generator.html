What Makes A Great Caption?

Captions are the second-biggest hook after your first frame. Here's how to nail them.

🎣 Hook in the first line

Put the most interesting part first so people stay to read and engage before tapping away.

💬 Start a conversation

Ask a question or invite an opinion. Comments boost your video in the algorithm.

😀 Match your vibe

Humour, inspiration or storytelling — pick the tone that matches your niche and audience.

📣 End with a CTA

Tell people to follow, share or comment so they know exactly what to do next.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should captions be long or short?

Short and punchy usually works best. If you go long, keep the first line as the hook.

Do captions help the algorithm?

Yes. Good captions increase watch time and engagement, the two things TikTok ranks on.

Can I edit the generated caption?

Of course. Treat the generated caption as a starting point and make it your own.
